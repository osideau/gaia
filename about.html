About Gaia

Healing that begins
where you are.

We believe the natural world is a powerful co-therapist. Gaia Mental Health was founded on the understanding that healing happens not just in the mind — but in the body, in relationship, and in the landscape around us.

Our Mission

Grounded in evidence.
Inspired by nature.

Gaia Mental Health offers psychotherapy that integrates relational, somatic, cognitive, trauma-informed, and nature-based approaches. Our work is guided by warmth, curiosity, and deep respect for each person's unique path.

We offer therapy in outdoor settings across Ontario — on trails, near water, and in places where the natural world can be part of the therapeutic process.

What We Stand For

Our values shape everything.

These aren't just words on a wall — they inform how we show up in every session, every relationship, every interaction.

01

Safety First

We create space where clients feel safe to explore difficult experiences at their own pace, without pressure or judgment.

02

Whole-Person Care

We work with the full person — mind, body, nervous system, story, and relationships — not just presenting symptoms.

03

Connection to Land

We believe the natural world has its own healing intelligence. We integrate nature as a co-facilitator, not just a backdrop.

04

Cultural Humility

We approach each person's background, identity, and worldview with curiosity, respect, and a commitment to anti-oppressive practice.

05

Authentic Relationship

Therapy works best when the therapeutic relationship is genuine. We show up as real, present humans — not blank screens.

06

Ongoing Learning

We are committed to continued growth, training, and supervision to ensure our practice remains evidence-informed and ethical.
