Therapy Services

Where healing
meets the outdoors.

We offer individual, group, and nature-based therapy experiences across Ontario. Each modality is tailored to your needs, your pace, and your story.

Individual Therapy

Hike & Talk
Therapy

Walk alongside your therapist in a natural setting — trails, parks, or waterside paths. Movement and nature work together to reduce anxiety, open up conversation, and help you process what matters most.

Research shows walking side-by-side reduces the intensity of eye contact, making it easier to speak about difficult topics. Nature adds a regulatory quality that static indoor settings can't replicate.

Experiential Therapy

Canoe
Therapy

Experience therapy on the water. The rhythmic motion of paddling, the sound of water, and the open sky create a deeply regulating environment — ideal for clients dealing with trauma, dissociation, or emotional dysregulation.

Canoe therapy is offered in a contained, supervised setting. No prior paddling experience is required.

Individual Therapy

Reflection &
Growth Sessions

Sit together in nature — a quiet clearing, a bench by the water, a sheltered outdoor space — and move through deeper therapeutic work at your own pace. These sessions blend traditional talk therapy with the grounding quality of outdoor presence.

Available for clients who want the benefits of nature without the physical demands of walking or paddling.

Group Therapy

Group
Experiences

Heal in community. Our group experiences bring together small cohorts of 6–8 people in carefully facilitated outdoor settings. Groups are themed around specific experiences: grief, burnout recovery, anxiety, and seasonal well-being.

Group therapy offers something individual therapy cannot — the experience of being truly witnessed by others who understand.
