The Approach

Support for the whole person.

Therapy is grounded in the understanding that healing involves more than changing thoughts — it also involves restoring safety, regulation, connection, embodiment, and relationship.

Somatic

Body-based and nervous system-focused therapy

Supporting grounding, embodiment, nervous system regulation, and awareness of the connection between mind and body.

Cognitive

Mindfulness and thought-pattern awareness

Exploring thinking, self-talk, attention, stress, and behaviour through mindfulness and CBT-informed approaches.

Relational

Emotion-focused and relationship-centered therapy

Therapy grounded in authenticity, emotional awareness, attachment, connection, and meaningful human relationships.

Nature-Based

Movement, outdoor therapy, and connection to the natural world

Integrating walk-and-talk therapy, outdoor environments, grounding, movement, and connection to land.

Trauma-Informed

Safety, pacing, and compassionate healing

Creating therapy that prioritizes safety, capacity, collaboration, empowerment, and respect for each person’s experiences.
