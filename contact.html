The Process

What to expect.

Starting therapy can feel like a big step. Here's exactly what happens when you reach out.

01

You reach out

Fill out the form above, send an email, or give us a call. There's no commitment required — just a conversation.

02

Free 20-minute consultation

We'll connect by phone or video to hear a bit about what's going on for you, answer your questions, and discuss the right service and therapist fit.

03

We confirm your first session

Once we've matched you with a therapist and service, we'll confirm your first outdoor session — including location, what to bring, and what to expect.

04

You show up as you are

No preparation required. No right way to feel. Just arrive, and we'll take it from there — together, at your pace.
