Why Being Near Water
Changes Everything

There is something that happens at the water's edge that is difficult to name but impossible to ignore. A kind of settling. The body softens, the breath slows, and the mind — for perhaps the first time all day — stops rehearsing the future or replaying the past. This is not coincidence. It is biology.
