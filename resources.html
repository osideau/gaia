Somatic Practices

Working with your body.

These practices can be done anywhere — on a walk, in your car, or at your desk. They are designed to help you return to a sense of safety in your own body.

01

Orienting

Slowly look around the room you're in. Notice five things you can see. Let your eyes rest on each one briefly. This simple act signals to your nervous system that you are safe and present — it interrupts the stress response and anchors you in the here and now.

02

Grounded Feet

Place both feet flat on the floor. Feel the weight of your legs, the pressure through your soles. Press gently downward and notice the earth pushing back. This simple contact with the ground can interrupt a spiral and return you to the present moment.

03

Sigh of Relief

Take a deep breath in through the nose — then add one more small sip of air at the top. Release slowly through the mouth. This "double inhale" fully expands the lungs and activates the parasympathetic nervous system more powerfully than a standard deep breath.

04

Safe Place Visualization

Close your eyes and bring to mind a place — real or imagined — where you feel completely safe and at ease. Notice what you see, hear, smell, and feel there. Spend two to three minutes in this place whenever you need to resource yourself between sessions.
